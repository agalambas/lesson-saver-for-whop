Lesson Saver for Whop · Version 1.2.0 · Updated 28 September 2026

Privacy policy

Publisher: Alexandre Galambas. Support: GitHub Issues.

Lesson Saver for Whop is an independent browser extension that lets you select and save videos you have permission to download from the Whop lesson you are viewing. The extension does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising system, or publisher-operated download server.

Information the extension handles

  • The current tab's URL, to verify that it is a Whop page before scanning.
  • Video titles, playback identifiers, media URLs, and download status, to display and maintain your selected download list.
  • Signed playback URLs, which may contain access tokens already provided by the video player, to request your selected videos.
  • Video and audio bytes, to assemble downloads and combine separate audio and video tracks on your device.
  • The IDs and status of downloads started by the extension, to display completion and support stopping downloads.

The extension does not read account passwords, use the cookies API, enumerate your browser history, or collect analytics. It does not scan unrelated websites or inspect private messages or payment forms. Selected videos may themselves contain personal information.

Where processing and storage happen

Download-list metadata is stored locally in your Chrome profile. Signed playback URLs are held in Chrome's in-memory extension session storage; they are cleared when Chrome restarts or the extension is reloaded, disabled, or updated. The persistent list does not contain those full signed links. Old versions' stored links are migrated out of the persistent list when this version opens.

Media processing takes place on your device using a bundled processor. There is no upload to a publisher server, no cloud account for the extension, and no Chrome storage.sync use. Only open the extension in a browser profile and on a device you trust.

Network requests and service providers

To download a selected video, the extension sends HTTPS requests directly to the source Whop or Mux media service. Signed playback tokens are sent as part of those requests where required. Like other network requests, the source service receives your IP address, requested resource, and normal request metadata. The extension does not control the source provider's own privacy practices or the website's analytics. Native Chrome downloads may apply the browser's normal authentication and cookie behavior; the extension does not read those cookies.

No user data is sent to the extension publisher by the extension. The publisher does not sell extension user data or use it for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

Retention and deletion

Remove a list item to delete its stored metadata and current session link. Clear completed removes finished entries. Uninstalling the extension clears its extension storage. Restarting Chrome clears session links, but keeps the list so you can rescan unfinished lessons. Files you have downloaded remain in your Downloads folder until you delete them yourself. Chrome may retain its normal download-history records separately.

Limited Use

The use of information received through this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is handled only to provide the video selection, local list, and download functions described here.

Support and policy changes

Use the support contact shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing to contact its publisher. If you choose to send a support request, its contents are provided voluntarily through the support channel you use; the extension itself sends no support messages or diagnostic reports. Do not include passwords or signed playback links in public support requests.

Changes to the extension's data practices will be reflected in this policy. The date above identifies the latest revision.
